Hermes Agent Google Cloud App

Hermes is the personal AI assistant of Christopher Baek. This app allows Hermes to connect to the Google account of its sole authorized user — Christopher Baek — so that at his request it can read and manage his own Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, and Contacts.

This application is operated by its user and for its user, as a single-user personal tool. It is not offered to the general public.
